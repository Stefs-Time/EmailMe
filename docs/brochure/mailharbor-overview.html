Deliver & protect

Reach the inbox. Protect your reputation.

A sending engine that plays by the rules Gmail, Yahoo and Microsoft expect, so your domain stays trusted.

Smart sending engine

  • Sends through your own SMTP server at the pace you set, with an optional hourly cap
  • Temporary failures retried automatically (5, 30 and 120 minutes)
  • Mail server down? The campaign pauses itself, alerts you and resumes without losing anyone
  • Every person is re-checked against the suppression list a second before their email goes
  • Never sends the same email twice, even after a restart

Unsubscribe done right

Every email carries an unsubscribe link and the one-click unsubscribe headers that Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders. Unsubscribe, preferences, stay-subscribed and view-online pages are built in: MailHarbor creates each person's link when it sends.

Bounce intelligence from three sources

Rejections at send time, your bounce mailbox and your provider's webhook are classified automatically:

SignalExampleWhat MailHarbor does
Hard bounce550 5.1.1 user unknownSuppresses the address straight away
Soft bounce452 mailbox fullCounts it and retries; suppresses after the limit you set
Policy / spam block550 5.7.1 blockedHolds the contact for review instead of wrongly removing them
ComplaintMarked as spamSuppresses immediately and permanently

A suppression list nothing can bypass

Imports, list changes and manual edits can never quietly re-subscribe someone who unsubscribed, bounced or complained. Only an administrator can reactivate a contact, and only with a recorded reason and new consent.
