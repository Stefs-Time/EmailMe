# MailHarbor introduction & permission email (internal)

> **Internal.** Copy for introducing MailHarbor to prospective customers from our own MailHarbor instance. Start a
> campaign from the **Introduction & permission request** sample and replace the [bracketed] text with the copy below.
> Customers see only the generic sample.

## Before you send

- **Audience:** people who aren't customers and haven't agreed to our emails, e.g. business cards or event contacts.
  Import them with consent method **Other**, and record where each address came from in the evidence ("Business card,
  [event], [date]").
- **POPIA s69(2):** we may ask each person **once**, and not at all if they've already said no (suppressed contacts
  are excluded automatically). **Never re-send** to people who didn't answer.
- **Afterwards:** send marketing only to a segment with *Consent method* = *Permission email* and status *Eligible*.
  Everyone else gets nothing more.
- **Content:** keep the email an introduction plus the question. A full sales pitch to people without consent is
  itself direct marketing. Have the information officer approve the final text.

## Copy

**Subject:** Introducing MailHarbor: may we keep in touch?
**Preview text:** Email marketing your team owns, on your own mail server. One question: may we email you?

**Heading:** Hello {{ first_name | "there" }}, may we introduce ourselves?

**Introduction:**
> We're **MailHarbor**. We help South African organisations run email marketing they own: campaigns sent through
> **your own mail server**, consent and unsubscribes handled for POPIA, and clear reports. It runs as a fully managed
> service, with your own private instance and database.
>
> [Why you're writing: e.g. It was good to meet you at [event]. You mentioned [their challenge], and we think
> MailHarbor could help.]

**Benefit one: Your sending, your reputation**
> Emails go out through your own SMTP server at the speed you set. No shared IP pools and no per-contact pricing.

**Benefit two: POPIA built in**
> Consent records, one-click unsubscribe, a preference centre and a suppression list nothing can bypass.

**Permission box:**
> **May we keep in touch?**
> We'd like to email you about MailHarbor: product news, practical email-marketing tips and occasional offers, about
> once a month. We'll only do so if you say yes, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

**Button:** Yes, you may contact me *(already set to "Give permission")*

**Footer text:**
> Not interested? No thanks, don't contact me again. If you don't answer, we won't send you marketing emails.
> We received your details from [your business card at [event] / your company's website]. Questions about your
> information? Reply to this email.

Attach or link the product overview PDF only for people who said yes, in the first follow-up campaign.
