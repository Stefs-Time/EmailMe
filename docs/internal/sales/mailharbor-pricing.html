How pricing works

Three parts. No surprises.

You pay once to have MailHarbor set up for your organisation, then a monthly fee for hosting and a monthly plan for support and new features. MailHarbor never charges per email or per user.

from R34,500 once-off

Once-off. Your private MailHarbor is set up, branded and connected for your organisation, your contacts are brought in, and your team is trained. Part of this fee funds the platform you are joining.

Your own instance and private database, run and watched by us: computing resources, 24/7 monitoring, daily off-site backups, security updates and new versions. Sized to your contacts and sending volume.

A monitored helpdesk for your team, fixes, and the continued development of MailHarbor: every new feature and enhancement we release, plus time for improvements you ask for.

Terms in brief

Your platform. Installation sets up MailHarbor for your organisation and gives you the right to use it, with your own data, for as long as your hosting continues. Improvements we build, including those you request, become part of MailHarbor for every customer, which keeps development costs shared and low.

Your data. Your contacts and campaigns are yours. When you leave, we export them with your consent and unsubscribe history, then delete your service and backups. We act as your POPIA operator; the hosting region is set out in your agreement.

Term. 12 months, then month to month. One Development & Support plan is required with hosting: the helpdesk is how issues reach us.

Prices. In South African rand, excluding 15% VAT, valid for 30 days. Monthly fees are adjusted once a year by CPI. Hosting depends partly on US-dollar infrastructure costs; if the rand moves more than 10% against the dollar, we may review hosting fees with 30 days' notice.
